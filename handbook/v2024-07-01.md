# Employee Handbook

Edition effective 2024-07-01. Synthetic data generated by `asofrag seed`.

## 1. Conduct

### Vendor gifts {#CONDUCT-1.1}

Employees may accept a gift from a vendor worth up to $75 in a calendar year. Anything above that value must be declined or reported to the compliance team.

### Outside business interests {#CONDUCT-1.2}

Employees must disclose an outside business interest within 10 days of taking it on. The compliance team reviews each disclosure and replies in writing.

### Harassment complaints {#CONDUCT-1.3}

Human resources acknowledges a harassment complaint within 2 business days. An investigator with no reporting line to either party is assigned to the case.

### Social media {#CONDUCT-1.4}

Staff must not post confidential client names on social media accounts. Personal accounts may state the employer but must not speak for it.

### Privacy training {#CONDUCT-1.5}

Every new hire completes data privacy training within 30 days of joining. Refresher courses are assigned each January.

### Retaliation {#CONDUCT-1.7}

Retaliation against a person who reports a concern in good faith is grounds for dismissal. Managers must pass every report to human resources the same day.

### Relatives {#CONDUCT-1.8}

Relatives may not work in the same reporting line. Existing cases are reviewed by the compliance team.

## 2. Holidays

### Public holidays {#HOLIDAY-2.1}

The company observes 11 paid public holidays each calendar year, listed in the annual calendar. A holiday that falls on a weekend moves to the nearest weekday.

### Floating holidays {#HOLIDAY-2.2}

Employees choose 2 floating holidays per year to use on days of personal significance. Unused floating holidays lapse on 31 December.

### Holiday pay {#HOLIDAY-2.3}

Work on a public holiday is paid at 2 times the base rate. The pay applies to the hours worked on the day only.

### Holiday cover {#HOLIDAY-2.4}

Teams publish their holiday coverage rota 3 weeks before each public holiday. Rota swaps are agreed between colleagues.

### Year-end closure {#HOLIDAY-2.5}

The offices close between 25 December and 1 January. Staff who must work in that period receive an extra day off in lieu.

### Anniversary half day {#HOLIDAY-2.6}

Employees receive a half day off on their work anniversary. The half day must be taken within the same month.

### Early closing {#HOLIDAY-2.7}

Offices close at 3 pm on the last working day before a public holiday. Customer support continues on a reduced rota.

## 3. Leave

### Vacation accrual {#LEAVE-3.1}

Full-time employees accrue 20 days of paid vacation per year. Accrual is prorated for part-time staff.

### Parental leave {#LEAVE-3.2}

Employees receive 12 weeks of paid parental leave after the birth or adoption of a child. The leave may be taken in one block or in two blocks within the first year.

### Sick leave {#LEAVE-3.3}

Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year. A medical certificate is required after three consecutive days.

### Bereavement leave {#LEAVE-3.4}

Employees receive 3 days of paid bereavement leave on the death of an immediate family member. Additional unpaid time may be agreed with the manager.

### Jury duty {#LEAVE-3.5}

Employees called for jury duty keep full pay for up to 10 days of jury service. The court summons must be shown to human resources.

### Carry-over cap {#LEAVE-3.6}

Employees may carry over up to 8 unused vacation days into the next year. Days above the cap are forfeited on 31 March.

### Sabbatical {#LEAVE-3.7}

Employees with seven years of service may apply for an unpaid sabbatical of up to 3 months. The role is held open for the whole period.

### Military leave {#LEAVE-3.8}

Reservists called to active duty are granted unpaid leave and keep their seniority. Benefits continue for 6 months.

### Volunteer time {#LEAVE-3.9}

Employees may spend 16 paid hours a year volunteering with a registered charity. The charity must be approved by the community team.

## 4. Expenses

### Meal per diem {#EXPENSE-4.1}

The daily meal per diem on overnight business trips is $55. The per diem is paid without receipts.

### Receipt threshold {#EXPENSE-4.2}

A receipt is required for any single expense above $25. Lost receipts need a signed statement from the claimant.

### Submission deadline {#EXPENSE-4.3}

Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the end of the trip. Late reports are paid in the following month.

### Client dinners {#EXPENSE-4.4}

Dinner with a client is reimbursed up to $80 per person. The most senior attendee pays and submits the claim.

### Corporate cards {#EXPENSE-4.5}

Corporate cards carry a monthly limit of $5000. Personal purchases on the card are repaid within five days.

### Airfare class {#EXPENSE-4.6}

Flights shorter than six hours are booked in economy class. Longer flights may be booked in premium economy.

### Trade press {#EXPENSE-4.7}

The company reimburses up to $30 a month for trade press subscriptions. Claims are submitted with the monthly invoice.

### Tips {#EXPENSE-4.8}

Tips of up to 15 percent of a restaurant bill are reimbursed. Tips above that need approval from a manager.

## 5. Remote work

### Remote days {#REMOTE-5.1}

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. The remaining days are spent in the office with the team.

### Internet stipend {#REMOTE-5.2}

Remote employees receive a home internet stipend of $40 per month. The stipend is added to the monthly payroll.

### Core hours {#REMOTE-5.3}

Remote employees must be reachable during core hours from 10:00 to 15:00 local time. Meetings are not scheduled outside core hours.

### VPN {#REMOTE-5.4}

Work on company systems from home requires the VPN to be connected. Access from a personal device needs the approval of the security team.

### Chair allowance {#REMOTE-5.5}

A one-time ergonomic chair allowance of $250 is available to remote employees. The chair stays with the employee.

### Working abroad {#REMOTE-5.6}

Remote work from another country is limited to 30 days a year. Longer periods need approval from the tax and legal teams.

### Workspace safety {#REMOTE-5.7}

Remote employees complete a home workspace safety checklist at onboarding. The checklist is renewed every two years.

## 6. Travel

### Hotel cap {#TRAVEL-6.1}

Hotel stays are reimbursed up to $180 per night in standard cities. Higher caps apply in cities on the premium list.

### Mileage {#TRAVEL-6.2}

Business mileage in a personal car is reimbursed at $0.70 per mile. Commuting miles are not reimbursed.

### Booking lead time {#TRAVEL-6.3}

Flights are booked at least 14 days before departure. Exceptions need the approval of the department head.

### Rental cars {#TRAVEL-6.4}

Rental cars are booked in the compact or mid-size class. Insurance is purchased through the corporate rental agreement.

### Airport parking {#TRAVEL-6.5}

Airport parking is reimbursed at the long-stay rate of up to $22 per day. Valet parking is not reimbursed.

### International travel {#TRAVEL-6.6}

Travel outside the home country needs written approval 4 weeks in advance. Approval covers the itinerary and the visa plan.

### Travel insurance {#TRAVEL-6.7}

Travel insurance is provided automatically for every approved business trip. Claims are filed with the insurer within 60 days.

### Loyalty points {#TRAVEL-6.8}

Loyalty points earned on business travel belong to the traveller. They may not be used to upgrade a company-paid ticket.

## 7. Overtime

### Overtime premium {#OVERTIME-7.1}

Overtime hours are paid at 1.50 times the regular hourly rate. Overtime applies to non-exempt employees only.

### Overtime approval {#OVERTIME-7.2}

Overtime must be approved by a manager before it is worked. Unapproved hours are paid but may lead to a warning.

### On-call stipend {#OVERTIME-7.3}

Employees on the on-call rota receive a stipend of $150 for each week on call. Call-outs are paid separately as overtime.

### Compensatory time {#OVERTIME-7.4}

Salaried employees may bank up to 24 hours of compensatory time. Banked hours expire after six months.

### Overtime meals {#OVERTIME-7.5}

A meal allowance of $20 is paid when overtime runs past eight in the evening. The allowance is claimed through the expense system.

### Weekend shifts {#OVERTIME-7.6}

Weekend shifts carry a premium of $60 per shift for hourly employees. The premium is separate from overtime.

### Rest periods {#OVERTIME-7.7}

Employees must have at least 11 hours of rest between two shifts. A shorter rest needs the written agreement of the employee.

## 8. Equipment

### Laptop refresh {#EQUIP-8.1}

Laptops are replaced every 3 years. Earlier replacement is granted when a device fails and cannot be repaired.

### Monitors {#EQUIP-8.2}

Each employee may order one external monitor costing up to $300. Additional monitors need a business case.

### Return of equipment {#EQUIP-8.3}

Equipment must be returned within 7 days of an employee's last working day. Unreturned equipment is deducted from final pay.

### Lost devices {#EQUIP-8.4}

A lost or stolen device must be reported to the security team within 24 hours. The device is remotely wiped on receipt of the report.

### Phone stipend {#EQUIP-8.5}

A monthly stipend of $35 covers the cost of a personal mobile phone used for work. The stipend is paid by direct deposit.

### Headsets {#EQUIP-8.6}

A headset is provided to every employee who takes video calls. Replacement headsets are issued once a year.

### Software licences {#EQUIP-8.7}

Software licences are requested through the IT service desk. Licences unused for 90 days are reclaimed.
